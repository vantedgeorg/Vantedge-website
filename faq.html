For firms

What is Vantedge?

Vantedge is building the talent infrastructure for finance: a proof-of-work platform where candidates do real finance work and defend it.

Why not just use CVs and interviews?

CVs show where someone studied, not what they can do. AI has made cover letters and online tests easy to fake. Real work that a candidate has to explain and defend is much harder to fake.

How does it work?

Candidates complete a finance case that looks like the job, such as a valuation or an investment memo, then explain and defend their work in a recorded interview.

What do firms get?

A profile for each candidate with the work itself and the recorded defence. You see who can do the job before you spend time interviewing.

Do you score or rank candidates?

No. No algorithm scores, ranks or rejects anyone. You see the work and the defence, and your firm makes the decision.

How is this different from online tests?

Most online tests are multiple choice or scored automatically, and both are easy for AI to game. We show you the work and the candidate explaining it in their own words.

Is Vantedge a recruitment agency?

No. We don't sell CVs or shortlists. We give firms evidence of ability.

Who is it for?

Investment Banking, Private Equity, Venture Capital, Wealth Management, Asset Management and Corporate Finance firms. We're starting with boutiques.

Is the platform live?

Not yet, and that is deliberate. We are building it alongside the firms who will use it, so it solves the problems they actually have. Firms that speak with us now help shape the product and are first to use it.

What happens if I book a call?

We ask how you hire juniors and what is hardest about it. No pitch, nothing to buy. Book a free call.

For candidates

How do I get involved?

Join the waitlist. You'll be among the first to hear when we open.

What kind of work will I do?

Cases that look like a junior's first weeks: valuations, investment memos and deal work, based on public information.

What is the defence?

A recorded interview where you explain your work and the decisions behind it. It's your chance to show that the thinking is yours.
