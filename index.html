Leadership and Board of Advisors

Board advisor Gregory Bournet Partner, PwC Malaysia, Corporate Finance and M&A

Gregory leads the Corporate Finance and Mergers & Acquisitions practice at PwC across Malaysia and Vietnam. He has advised multinational corporations, private equity firms, sovereign wealth funds and family-owned businesses on transactions ranging from mid-market acquisitions to billion-dollar cross-border deals.

At Vantedge, Gregory works closely with the team as a strategic sounding board, drawing on his extensive experience in corporate finance and M&A to provide practical perspectives, challenge ideas and share insights from real-world transactions. He also lends his expertise to the development of Vantedge's initiatives, helping ensure that our approach is grounded in industry realities and informed by the perspectives of an experienced practitioner.

Xuenna Aw Founder, Vantedge

Xuenna leads strategy, direction, and global expansion across Europe, APAC, and North America at Vantedge, building the talent infrastructure for finance to fix a broken early-career recruitment pipeline. Previously in Mergers and Acquisitions at PwC, she saw firsthand how outdated the barrier to entry is for aspiring analysts, driving her focus on replacing passive résumé screening with real-world proof of capability.

Mayur Tejwani Ex-McKinsey, JPMorgan | Chartered Accountant & Esade MBA

Mayur Tejwani is a strategy and M&A professional with a Chartered Accountant qualification and an MBA from Esade. He leads M&A and value creation at a deeptech healthcare company in Barcelona. Prior to that, he spent more than four years at McKinsey advising banks and corporates on strategy, investment banking, and sustainability, and two years at JPMorgan building financial models. Most at home where a valuation has to hold up in front of a buyer, he works across Europe and the GCC. At Vantedge, he leverages his consulting and strategy background to support our team by stress-testing and validating our ideas and execution.
