Frequently asked questions
What is the purpose of the call?

To understand how your firm hires interns and analysts today, and where the process could work better. Your insights inform how we build the platform.

Is there any cost or commitment?

No. The call is complimentary and carries no obligation.

How should I prepare?

No preparation is needed. A general sense of how your firm recruits junior talent is enough.

How will the information I share be used?

Everything discussed is treated as confidential. It is used only to inform the development of our platform and is never shared in a way that identifies your firm.

What happens after the call?

Your firm will be among the first to access the platform and our talent pool when they launch.

May a colleague join the call?

Yes. You are welcome to include anyone involved in your firm's hiring.
